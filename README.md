# The Small Business IT Security Checklist: 25 Things to Check This Month

> **Who this is for:** Business owners, office managers, and anyone who "also handles IT."
> **Time needed:** About 60-90 minutes to review. Longer to fix gaps.

Most small business breaches don't involve movie-style hacking. They happen because of a missing update, a reused password, or a backup nobody tested.

This checklist covers the basics that prevent the most common problems. Copy it into your own repo or issue tracker and tick items off as you go.

---

## 1. Accounts and Passwords

- [ ] Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is **enabled on all email accounts**
- [ ] MFA is enabled on banking, accounting, and payroll tools
- [ ] MFA is required for **all admin accounts**
- [ ] The company uses a **password manager**
- [ ] No shared passwords for individual staff logins
- [ ] Former employees' accounts are **disabled** (not just forgotten)
- [ ] Admin rights are limited to people who need them

## 2. Backups

- [ ] Critical data is backed up **automatically**
- [ ] At least one backup copy is **offline or immutable** (ransomware can't alter it)
- [ ] Backups follow the **3-2-1 rule**: 3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite
- [ ] A **test restore** has been done in the last 90 days
- [ ] Someone is responsible for checking backup alerts

## 3. Devices and Software

- [ ] Operating systems and apps are **set to update automatically**
- [ ] Every computer has **endpoint protection** (EDR or reputable antivirus)
- [ ] Laptops use **disk encryption** (BitLocker or FileVault)
- [ ] Unsupported software (for example, end-of-life Windows versions) is **replaced**
- [ ] Lost or stolen devices can be **remotely wiped**

## 4. Email and Phishing

- [ ] Email filtering or security is turned on
- [ ] SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are configured for your domain
- [ ] Staff know how to **report suspicious emails**
- [ ] A **call-back rule** exists for payment or bank detail changes
- [ ] Inbox rules and forwarding settings are **reviewed periodically**

## 5. Network

- [ ] The firewall firmware is up to date
- [ ] Default router and device passwords have been **changed**
- [ ] Guest Wi-Fi is **separate** from the business network
- [ ] Remote access uses a **VPN or secure gateway**, not open ports

## 6. People and Policies

- [ ] Staff receive **security awareness training** at least yearly
- [ ] There is a written **acceptable use policy**, including AI tools
- [ ] A one-page **incident response plan** lists who to call
- [ ] **Onboarding and offboarding checklists** exist for IT access

---

## How to Score Yourself

| Checked | Rating | Next step |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| 21-25 | Strong | Keep testing and reviewing quarterly |
| 14-20 | Decent | Fix the unchecked items, starting with MFA and backups |
| 7-13 | At risk | Prioritize sections 1 and 2 this week |
| 0-6 | High risk | Get a professional security review soon |

## Where to Start if You're Overwhelmed

If you can only do **three things** this week:

1. Turn on **MFA** everywhere you can
2. **Test a backup restore**
3. Turn on **automatic updates**

These three steps address a large share of everyday risk.

## Make It a Habit

Security isn't a one-time project. Consider:

- Reviewing this checklist **quarterly**
- Opening a GitHub Issue for each unchecked item and assigning an owner
- Keeping a short changelog of what you've fixed
